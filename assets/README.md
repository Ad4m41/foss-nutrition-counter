# Asset provenance

`../logo.png` is the user-supplied source logo for Nutrition Counter: a cream plate with orange, yellow and green food segments on a dark green background. Keep this original raster as the source of truth.

`icon.png` is a 1024 × 1024 Lanczos resize of the source PNG. `favicon.png` is a 64 × 64 resize. `android-icon-foreground.png` places a 768 × 768 resize on a 1024 × 1024 dark green canvas to leave room for Android adaptive icon masks. `android-icon-monochrome.png` uses the cream outline and food symbols as a white alpha mask with the same padding for themed icons.

The initial scaffold and its original assets came from Expo's blank TypeScript template. The original MIT notice remains in `EXPO-TEMPLATE-LICENSE.txt`.
